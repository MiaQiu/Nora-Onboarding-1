---
name: Nora outcome claims
description: How to handle time-bound screen-time outcomes and coping observations responsibly.
---

Present a two-month screen-time outcome as a practice goal, not a guaranteed end to tantrums, and state that progress varies between children.

**Why:** The user requested an expected two-month outcome, but no evidence was supplied supporting a guaranteed result. The implementation deliberately avoids promising a fixed behavioral result.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction when revising plans or promotional copy. Do not silently turn a goal into a guarantee.

Treat the hidden-signal answers as parent observations, not a diagnostic assessment.

**Why:** The user requested questions about coping behaviors, not clinical scoring or diagnosis.

**How to apply:** Do not infer a disorder or claim these behaviors prove poor coping without a separately reviewed assessment.
