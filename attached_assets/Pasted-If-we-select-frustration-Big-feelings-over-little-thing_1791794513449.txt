Have you heard of temperament?
Every child has a natural way of responding to the world. These differences are part of temperament. Some children feel emotions more intensely. Others find it harder to adapt when things don't go as expected or take longer to settle after becoming upset. These differences can influence how children respond to frustration. It's not your child's fault, and it's not your fault either.
