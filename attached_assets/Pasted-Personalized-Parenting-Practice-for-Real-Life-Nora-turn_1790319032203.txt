Meltdowns when screens end are one of the most common struggles parents face, and they're genuinely hard on everyone.  
Here's what's happening: screens are incredibly stimulating—they deliver constant novelty and excitement in a way that few other activities can match. This makes stopping genuinely hard for many kids. 
When the stimulation suddenly stops, their nervous system has to shift gears quickly, and that shift can feel really uncomfortable.
